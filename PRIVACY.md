# Privacy

Fruit Picker is a local, skills-only plugin. It does not operate a
developer-hosted server, create user accounts, collect telemetry, or send
photos and answers to the plugin author.

The plugin instructs the user's AI agent to assess images and observations in
the user's current AI product or local workspace. The AI product or runtime
used to execute the plugin may process prompts and images under that provider's
own privacy terms.

The plugin author does not receive, store, sell, or share personal data through
the plugin.

Questions may be filed at
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Ezra-Y/fruit-picker/issues).
