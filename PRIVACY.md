# Privacy

Fruit Picker is a local, skills-only plugin. It does not operate a
developer-hosted server, create user accounts, collect telemetry, or send
photos and answers to the plugin author.

## Data processed

The plugin may process only the images and observations the user chooses to
provide, such as fruit photos, cultivar information, aroma, firmness, stem
condition, and the user's buying question.

## Purpose and recipients

This data is used only to assess visible fruit characteristics, ask relevant
follow-up questions, and provide a buying recommendation with stated limits.
Processing occurs in the user's selected AI product or local workspace. That
AI product or runtime may receive prompts and images under its own privacy
terms. The plugin author does not receive the data, and the plugin does not
send it to a developer-hosted service, advertising network, or data broker.

## Retention

The plugin author retains no user data. Local images, notes, and generated
outputs remain under the user's control until the user deletes them. Any
retention by the selected AI product or runtime is governed by that provider's
terms and account settings.

## User controls

Users choose which images and observations to provide, may stop the assessment
at any time, and may delete local inputs and outputs. The plugin has no separate
account, tracking identifier, or author-controlled data store.

The plugin author does not sell or share personal data through the plugin.
Questions may be filed at
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Ezra-Y/fruit-picker/issues).
